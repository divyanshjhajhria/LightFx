# Checks for a person with eyes and ears

Everything here has never been seen or heard by an automated check. Tick each one before the demo.

## Sound

- [ ] The voice-over speaks every step of the guided demo, in a clear English voice, at a volume the room can hear.
- [ ] With the network off, the voice still speaks (the app now prefers a voice installed on the machine).
- [ ] M mutes it at once; pressing M again brings it back on the next line.

## Motion

- [ ] Switching between Overview and the other tabs: the bottom panel grows or shrinks smoothly over a fifth of a
      second, and the solar system re-fits with no jump and no blank flash.
- [ ] The gauge markers on the Overview cards slide when the figures change; in the panic, the old-news bank's
      marker visibly crosses into the red end.
- [ ] Opening and closing a desk panel: the solar system eases aside and back.
- [ ] With "Reduce motion" turned on in the operating system, nothing animates.

## Hand and keyboard

- [ ] Real mouse hover on each dock control shows its one-sentence tooltip after a short pause.
- [ ] Tab from the top of the page reaches every control in a sensible order, each with a visible outline, and the
      dock tooltips appear on focus.
- [ ] More opens with the keyboard, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the More button.
- [ ] During the guided demo: dragging cannot select text, the page cannot scroll, and pinch or Ctrl-scroll does
      not zoom the page.
- [ ] R restarts at once; G starts the demo; Escape leaves it.

## Browsers and screens

- [ ] Safari: the guided demo, every tab, the survival map.
- [ ] Firefox: the same.
- [ ] A phone held upright: the page stacks, nothing runs off the side, the trade ticket can be used.
- [ ] A projector at 1280x720 and a large screen at 1920x1080: nothing overlaps.

## People

- [ ] Someone who has never seen it watches the guided demo once, then says in a sentence what it showed. If they
      cannot, note where they lost the thread.

## Left over from earlier steps

- [ ] The guided demo opened with `?demo&agents=replay` and a real recorded session: "The agents" tab appears at the
      isolation step, and every caption is still true. (Needs a recorded model session first.)
- [ ] A live model session: the agents decide within a few seconds, their thoughts read well, and "Save this session"
      downloads a file that replays.
- [ ] Notes drawn as text on the canvas when "Show all desk voices" is on and three or fewer are in flight.
- [ ] The agents tab and the Overview on a narrow screen.
- [ ] The survival map and the agents tab on a phone.
- [ ] The error panel: in a development build open `?crash`, see "Something went wrong. Press R to restart the
      demo.", and press R.
